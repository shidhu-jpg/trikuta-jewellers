Meet the Founder

From the workbench to a trusted manufacturer

Trikuta Fine Jewels is run by Gaurav, a jeweller who has spent more than seven years learning the trade in the lanes of Chandni Chowk. He started at the workbench — watching karigars, learning how gold is melted, shaped and finished, and understanding why purity matters more than shine.

Today our workshop in Kinari Bazar, Chandni Chowk manufactures gold, silver and diamond jewellery for retailers, resellers and families. Being the manufacturer means we control every step — design, casting, stone setting, polishing and quality checks — under one roof.

Gaurav brings what many old shops miss: a young eye for modern designs, quick replies on WhatsApp and transparent pricing — while keeping the traditional craftsmanship Kinari Bazar is famous for.
